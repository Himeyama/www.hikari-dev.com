title: A simple solution to the problem of converting 0.248 (16) to a decimal fraction (Fundamental Information Technology Engineer Examination)
---

## Points
Hexadecimal problems use binary.

## Steps
1. Convert the hexadecimal number to binary.
   0.248<sub>(16)</sub> = 0.0010 0100 1000<sub>(2)</sub>

2. Remove the decimal point.
   0.0010 0100 1000<sub>(2)</sub>
  = 0.0010 0100 1000<sub>(2)</sub> × 0010 0000 0000<sub>(2)</sub> / 0010 0000 0000<sub>(2)</sub>
  = 0100 1001<sub>(2)</sub> / 0010 0000 0000<sub>(2)</sub>

3. Convert the binary number to decimal.
   0100 1001<sub>(2)</sub> / 0010 0000 0000<sub>(2)</sub>
  = (64 + 8 + 1) / 512
  = 73 / 512
